Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 19, 2026

Overview

This is a personal portfolio website for Darshan D G. It does not have a server or database of its own — it's a static site, and the only way it collects any information from you is if you choose to submit the contact form.

Information collected via the contact form

If you fill out and submit the contact form, the name, email address, subject, and message you enter are sent directly to a third-party form delivery service, Web3Forms, which forwards them to Darshan's email inbox. This site does not store that information itself — it has no database. Web3Forms' handling of that data is governed by their own privacy policy.

That information is used only to respond to your message. It is not sold, shared with advertisers, or used for marketing.

Cookies and tracking

This site does not set any cookies and does not use any analytics or advertising trackers of its own.

Third-party services this site loads

To render fonts, icons, and animations, this site loads a small number of resources from third-party content delivery networks. Visiting this site causes your browser to make requests to:

  • Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com) — for the site's typeface
  • Cloudflare CDN / cdnjs (cdnjs.cloudflare.com) — for icon fonts and animation libraries
  • Web3Forms (web3forms.com) — only if you submit the contact form

Each of these services may log standard technical information (such as your IP address and browser type) as part of normal web delivery, under their own respective privacy policies. This site does not control or have access to that data.

Hosting

This site is hosted on Vercel, which may log standard access data (such as IP address, request time, and user agent) as part of its infrastructure operation, under Vercel's privacy policy.

The chat assistant ("Sara")

The chat widget on this site runs entirely in your browser and does not send your messages anywhere — it just matches your questions against a small built-in list of answers about Darshan. Nothing you type into it leaves your device.
